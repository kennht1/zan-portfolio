01 — About

Learning by building, not by memorizing.

I'm Fauzan Naufal Satria — most people call me Zan. I'm a high school student in Indonesia who got into technology the messy way: by trying things, breaking them, and figuring out why they broke.

Instead of only watching tutorials, I build small projects to understand how things actually work — a chatbot that talks to an API, a script that measures network latency, a page that finally looks right after the tenth CSS rewrite. Each project is a way to learn something concrete.

Right now I'm focused on computer networking, web development, AI, and cybersecurity — and I'm still at the beginning of that journey. That's the point.
